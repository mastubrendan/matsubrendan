# Supply Chain Sourcing and Production Optimization

## Business Problem

Develop a 30-day production and sourcing plan that maximizes profit while satisfying customer demand and operational constraints.

---

## Objective

Determine optimal production and procurement quantities.

---

## Tools

- Excel Solver
- Linear Programming

---

## Constraints

- Demand
- Production capacity
- Inventory
- Storage
- Reserve requirements
- Procurement options

---

## Results

- Optimized order quantities
- 100% demand fulfillment
- Maximum modeled profit
