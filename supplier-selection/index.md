          <h1>
            Supplier Selection & Dual-Sourcing Strategy
          </h1>


          <p class="project-lead">

            I independently evaluated four potential wheel suppliers
            for a new vehicle launch and developed a sourcing
            recommendation based on quality, total cost, lead time,
            capacity and flexibility, supplier risk, and location.

            Rather than selecting a supplier based only on price,
            I built a weighted scorecard to evaluate the broader
            operational tradeoffs and developed a dual-sourcing
            strategy to reduce supply risk.

          </p>

            <div class="case-main">


              <div class="panel">

                <p class="eyebrow">
                  Evaluation Framework
                </p>


                <h2>
                  Building the supplier scorecard
                </h2>


                <p>

                  I structured the sourcing decision around six criteria
                  that represented both purchasing cost and operational
                  performance.

                  Quality received the largest weighting because the
                  product was positioned as a premium vehicle and supplier
                  quality therefore carried greater strategic importance.

                </p>


                <ul class="clean-list">

                  <li>
                    Quality — 30%
                  </li>

                  <li>
                    Total cost — 15%
                  </li>

                  <li>
                    Lead time — 15%
                  </li>

                  <li>
                    Capacity and flexibility — 15%
                  </li>

                  <li>
                    Supplier risk and experience — 15%
                  </li>

                  <li>
                    Location and logistics responsiveness — 10%
                  </li>

                </ul>


                <p>

                  Total cost incorporated piece price, tooling cost,
                  and transportation rather than comparing suppliers
                  using unit price alone.

                </p>

              </div>



              <div class="panel">

                <p class="eyebrow">
                  Supplier Analysis
                </p>


                <h2>
                  Comparing cost against operational capability
                </h2>


                <p>

                  The supplier comparison showed why the lowest-cost
                  option was not automatically the strongest purchasing
                  decision.

                  Each supplier presented a different combination of
                  cost, quality, lead time, available capacity,
                  logistics responsiveness, and uncertainty.

                </p>


                <ul class="clean-list">

                  <li>

                    Dragon Wheel & Tire provided existing FCA experience,
                    but its limited capacity, average quality rating,
                    and long transportation lead time weakened its
                    overall position.

                  </li>


                  <li>

                    Wally's Worldwide Wheels offered strong quality,
                    existing FCA experience, excellent logistics
                    responsiveness, and the ability to absorb a
                    20% increase in volume.

                  </li>


                  <li>

                    Ruota provided the strongest lead-time score and
                    experience producing products for premium automotive
                    brands, but carried greater capacity and supplier
                    experience risk.

                  </li>


                  <li>

                    Carnival offered the lowest modeled cost but had
                    unknown quality, no previous FCA experience,
                    and a 30-week tooling lead time.

                  </li>

                </ul>

              </div>



              <div class="panel">

                <p class="eyebrow">
                  Scorecard Result
                </p>


                <h2>
                  Wally ranked first, but the ranking was not the final decision
                </h2>


                <p>

                  Wally received the highest weighted score at 88.75,
                  driven primarily by strong quality, capacity,
                  supplier experience, low risk, and domestic
                  logistics responsiveness.

                </p>


                <p>

                  Ruota ranked behind Wally at 73.5 but provided
                  complementary advantages, particularly faster
                  tooling and production timing and experience
                  supporting premium automotive brands.

                </p>


                <p>

                  This created an opportunity to move beyond a
                  single-source decision and evaluate how the
                  suppliers could work together.

                </p>

              </div>



              <div class="panel">

                <p class="eyebrow">
                  Recommendation
                </p>


                <h2>
                  60% Wally / 40% Ruota dual sourcing
                </h2>


                <p>

                  I recommended allocating 60% of the sourcing volume
                  to Wally and 40% to Ruota.

                  Wally serves as the primary supplier because of its
                  strong quality, experience, reliability, and ability
                  to absorb additional volume.

                </p>


                <p>

                  Ruota provides a second qualified supply source with
                  faster lead-time characteristics and experience
                  serving premium automotive manufacturers.

                  The dual-source structure reduces dependence on a
                  single supplier while maintaining additional capacity
                  and flexibility if demand changes.

                </p>

              </div>



              <div class="panel">

                <p class="eyebrow">
                  Purchasing Takeaway
                </p>


                <h2>
                  Lowest price does not always mean lowest sourcing risk
                </h2>


                <p>

                  Carnival demonstrated the central tradeoff in the
                  analysis.

                  It offered the lowest modeled cost, but the long
                  tooling lead time, unknown quality, and lack of
                  previous FCA experience created risks that were
                  difficult to justify for a time-sensitive premium
                  product launch.

                </p>


                <p>

                  The project reinforced the importance of evaluating
                  suppliers across total cost, quality, delivery,
                  capacity, and risk rather than making purchasing
                  decisions from unit price alone.

                </p>

              </div>
