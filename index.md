# Workforce Scheduling Optimization

## Executive Summary

Developed a linear optimization model in Microsoft Excel Solver to generate cost-efficient weekly workforce schedules by minimizing total payroll hours while satisfying staffing requirements, employee availability, and operational constraints. This project demonstrates how mathematical optimization can improve labor planning and operational efficiency through data-driven scheduling decisions.

---

## Business Challenge

Retail workforce scheduling requires balancing labor costs with operational coverage while accounting for employee availability, staffing requirements, and scheduling restrictions. Manual scheduling often results in excess labor hours or insufficient staffing during key operating periods.

---

## Objective

Develop a linear optimization model that minimizes total weekly payroll hours while maintaining required staffing coverage and satisfying all scheduling constraints.

---

## Optimization Approach

The model applies Excel Solver and the Simplex LP algorithm to determine the optimal weekly workforce schedule.

### Decision Variables

- Employee shift assignments
- Daily scheduled labor hours
- Weekly labor allocation

### Objective Function

- Minimize total payroll hours

### Constraints

- Employee availability
- Minimum staffing requirements
- Maximum weekly labor hours
- Binary scheduling decisions
- Non-negative decision variables

---

## Business Outcome

The optimization model successfully generated an optimal weekly workforce schedule that:

- Minimized total payroll hours
- Maintained required staffing coverage
- Satisfied all scheduling constraints
- Demonstrated how optimization can support operational labor planning
